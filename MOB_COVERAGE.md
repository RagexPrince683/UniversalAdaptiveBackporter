# UAB mob support

The content ceiling is the **released Java Edition 1.21.11**, not snapshots or later releases. This catalog describes the maintained Forge **1.7.10** implementation. The 54 maintained mob entries have native implementations, including elder/juvenile variants and the command-only Illusioner. **Adapted** rows retain deliberately native AI, movement, presentation and compatible world rules. **Partially implemented** is reserved for concrete missing capabilities.

Status means source implementation coverage, separately from runtime acceptance. **Fully implemented** means the essential survival/AI path was found in the maintained implementation, with the stated legacy adaptations. **Adapted** identifies a functional legacy-compatible implementation. **Partially implemented** identifies a named missing mechanic; rate, spawn, navigation and presentation adaptations alone do not qualify. Runtime acceptance is reported separately and is not inferred from this classification. **Missing** means no maintained implementation was found. New entity classes alone do not qualify as complete.

## Forge 1.12.2 coverage

The native 1.12.2 port currently includes cod, salmon, tropical fish, pufferfish, drowned, phantoms, dolphins, turtles, axolotls and glow squid, alongside the mobs supplied by Minecraft 1.12.2 itself. Dolphins retain water steering, air/moisture needs, retaliation, item play, swimmer following, native Dolphin's Grace and guidance to saved treasure/sites/monuments. Guidance never generates terrain. Turtles retain beach spawning, seagrass breeding, saved home/egg state, return-and-lay behavior, egg incubation, baby growth and one-time scute maturation drops. Eggs retain count/crack states, Silk Touch and griefing-aware trampling; their small native collision hull allows actual walking/falling callbacks. Turtle helmets supply the retained above-water breathing reserve. Axolotls retain bucket NBT, tropical-fish breeding, variant inheritance, hunting, assisted-player regeneration and play-dead recovery. Glow squid retain native squid movement, synchronized damage-darkening and glow ink drops. Natural aquatic admission uses loaded-entity lifecycle counts, scaled shared/species budgets and local density; saved entities, commands, conversions, spawners and bucket release retain their normal joins. Native guardians keep the vanilla monster category, and optional squid limits affect only the exact vanilla squid class. Renderers use the maintained released geometry and textures. Every maintained mob family now has a native or adapted 1.12.2 implementation. Modern navigation and general swimming controls retain their documented engine boundaries; implementation coverage remains separate from complete gameplay acceptance.

Piglins, piglin brutes, hoglins, zoglins and striders also have native 1.12.2 implementations. Piglins retain gold-armor peace, group anger, admiration/bartering, crossbow equipment and conversion outside the Nether. Conversions retain equipment, name, age, health proportion and persistence. Hoglins breed with crimson fungi, avoid warped fungi and larger piglin groups, and convert to zoglins. Striders use an independent native lava spawn category, retain saddles, cold state, warped-fungus breeding and boosting, and consume native rider input on the server. Client vehicle coordinates do not own their movement. Brutes remain persistent structure/command inhabitants, including saved bastion encounters. Each species keeps its existing entity number and acquisition control. Native renderers retain model parts, baby scaling, held equipment and saddle overlays. Crossbows save their exact arrow ammunition across charging and world reloads; quick charge, multishot, piercing and native tipped/spectral effects are supported. Firework crossbow ammunition remains outside the maintained arrow-only subset. Modern sound registration and startup audio acquisition are now native; remaining sound replacements follow their own feature controls.

The 1.12.2 island also implements cats, pandas, wandering traders/trader llamas, allays, sniffers, armadillos, brown mooshrooms, bogged, parched, pillagers, ravagers, breezes and their equipment. Native village raids retain world-owned wave state and use the destination engine's evokers and vindicators. Sculk sensors/shriekers, the Warden, Darkness and Swift Sneak retain the maintained experimental boundaries; catalysts convert awarded native mob XP into saved, bounded spreading charge while retaining overflow as native XP.

Creaking Hearts retain log alignment, night activation, bound-entity identity, unload-safe replacement and resin production. Copper golems sort through an index of loaded native/copper chests, preserve held items and oxidation, and become poseable/revivable statues. Frog pregnancy produces frogspawn; tadpole buckets retain age/name/health, and maturation chooses the adult's climate variant. Camels/camel husks, happy ghasts and nautilus variants use native multiple-passenger lists, source equipment/abilities and server-owned movement. The 1.12.2 engine owns passenger saving and synchronization, so this target does not create legacy auxiliary seat entities. Dried ghasts advance hydration only in loaded game ticks; Breath of the Nautilus pauses oxygen consumption without refilling air.

Bees retain flight, flower/item tags, pollination, single-stage crop growth, anger/stinging, three-occupant hives, smoke-sensitive harvesting, honey and saved hive relocation. Native oak/birch growth and the maintained modern-tree paths can place populated nests. Foxes retain red/snow coats, trusted players, hunting/pouncing, sleep, berry gathering, mouth items, native food effects and breeding. Goats retain screaming variants, age-dependent horns, supported long jumps, prepared ramming and the eight horn instruments. Together with species supplied by 1.12.2, every new living species in the maintained roster now has a native implementation. Structure-specific acquisition, presentation details and optional integrations still have independent coverage boundaries. This section describes implementation coverage; it does not certify complete survival progression, multiplayer interaction or visual acceptance for every creature.

## Existing species, technical entities and exclusions

Zombie villagers, zombified piglins, wither skeletons, horses/donkeys/mules, skeleton/zombie horses, ocelots, wolves and mooshrooms already existed in 1.7.10. Later registry separation or renaming does not make them newly introduced species. EFR's zombie-villager and brown-mooshroom replacements are retained. New cold/warm animal appearances and wolf variants are variant work, not additional species. Zombie-horse natural jockey acquisition added in 1.21.11 is still a practical gameplay gap in the broader backport, tracked in the modern-content matrix; the existing horse entities are preserved.

Armor stands, boats/chest boats, rafts, minecarts, display/interaction entities, projectiles, item frames/glow item frames, markers, Mannequins (1.21.9), the fishing bobber and dropped items are **deliberately excluded from the mob count**. Required mob projectiles/equipment are dependencies, not excuses to add unrelated technical entities. Illusioners are included above because they are a released AI mob. Giants predate 1.7.10. Creaking Transient was a removed snapshot type, not a separate eligible released mob. Post-cutoff content, canceled mob-vote candidates and Bedrock-only NPC/agent types are excluded.

## Native acquisition and structures

Structure and biome distribution is adapted to the destination version. Modern spawn-rate fidelity does not justify replacing 1.7.10 terrain, cave or village systems.

New outposts use the released 15×21×15 watchtower with native palette/rotation adaptations, stairs, balconies, ominous wall banners, loot and pillagers. Saved surrounding allay cages, tents, logs and targets wait for their own chunks' first population. Steep or flooded pieces are skipped; already populated chunks and existing towers are unchanged. Evokers and vindicators remain available through raids. Patrols and traders use bounded player-local attempts. Ominous bottles start saved, finite raids at existing native villages, with difficulty-dependent waves and rewards; the villager engine is retained.

Modular Trial Chambers, normal/ominous vaults and keys now have an independent new-terrain path; see [WORLD_GENERATION.md](WORLD_GENERATION.md) for supported encounters and limitations. `enableMobDungeonContent` separately retains converted vanilla dungeons and their original six-mob reward/cooldown behavior. Ancient Cities require the experimental Sculk gate and provide a local Deep Dark cavern; no global cave-biome or world-height replacement is added.

Sniffer eggs and dried ghasts use native dungeon loot; dried ghasts also have their released crafting recipe. Copper/iron nautilus armor uses dungeons, gold/diamond armor uses native structure chests, and netherite armor uses the existing enabled smithing chain. No unrelated material substitutes are used for disabled prerequisites.

## Save, equipment and runtime boundaries

Existing local entity IDs 0–34 retain their meanings. New IDs append at 35–75 and remain registered when their new spawning switches are off. Technical entities 45 (trident), 52 (fangs), 55 (wind charge), 72 (passenger seat), 73 (spit), 74 (status arrow) and 75 (crossbow bolt) have no spawn eggs. No existing packet discriminator, biome identity or saved item meaning changes. Existing Stage B Nether/Pale Garden registration gates remain.

Fish and axolotl buckets preserve variant/name/health/age state as applicable; native bucket permissions and Forge events remain authoritative. Nether evaporation does not release an animal. Equipment, genes, owners, passengers, explored positions, raid progress and conversion timers use additive bounded NBT. Native registered seat entities preserve additional passengers through the ordinary Riding save path. Chest discovery tracks loaded chunk/tile lifecycle; neither sorting nor AI searches load distant chunks.

The ordinary owning server thread handles movement, equipment, inventory, combat, conversion and interactions. Native rider inputs steer mounts; no additional packet protocol is introduced. Happy-ghast harnesses can be sheared off; nautilus armor can be removed by sneaking with an empty hand. Sneaking with a tamed chested llama opens its strength-sized native inventory.

Water navigation uses bounded, collision-tested 3D steering instead of modern brain/navigation APIs. Natural aquatic admissions enforce species budgets, shared animal/hostile budgets and local school density. Named, tamed, bucket-released and other persistent animals do not consume natural budgets. A loaded-entity lifecycle index replaces repeated full-world counts for EFR water types. Optional strict vanilla-squid admission is disabled by default. Natural drowned require dark water, with less frequent and deeper ocean spawns; shallow exposed river water is night-only, while sufficiently dark/deep water remains eligible. Zombie conversion, commands and eggs bypass natural restrictions. There is no modern player-swimming pose, generalized waterlogging, global cave-biome system or world-height expansion. Dried Ghasts have local contained-water state and cities have local Deep Dark caverns. Dolphin’s Grace adjusts existing horizontal water drag; Breath of the Nautilus pauses oxygen consumption without refilling it.

Crossbows support saved charging, arrows, Quick Charge, Multishot and Piercing. Firework loading is absent. Tridents preserve the thrown stack and implement Loyalty, Channeling, Impaling and wet Riptide movement. They return an item on ordinary impact instead of embedding for pickup; Riptide does not implement modern swept spinning contact damage. Spears provide native server-authoritative extended thrust/mounted velocity damage without changing global player reach; Lunge adds its scoped server-owned movement ability. Native blocking, including held UAB shields, supplies the ravager stun interaction. Native combat timing and global damage ownership remain unchanged.

Retained native model geometry, textures, variants/equipment layers and core animation states are provided for the new mobs. Official ambient/hurt/death events use the existing AssetDirector path. Modern keyframe animation, every action sound, captain banners and offhand player poses are not reproduced completely. These presentation limits are independent of source-level survival mechanics.

## Reference boundary

Behavior and retained model geometry were inspected in Mojang’s official **1.21.11** client archive using its official client mappings. Textures are copied from that archive; sounds use the existing MCLib AssetDirector integration.

Release references: [Update Aquatic](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/update-aquatic-out-java), [1.20.5](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-20-5), [1.21](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21), [1.21.4](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21-4), [1.21.6](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21-6), [1.21.9](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21-9), and [1.21.11](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21-11). In particular Copper Golems, Camel Husks and Parched are within the cutoff. These references do not certify EFR runtime parity.

## Native Spring animal variants

Spring to Life keeps vanilla cow, pig, chicken, sheep and wolf identities and AI. Climate coats and sheep spawn colors use native biome information. Wolf coats/voices, breeding inheritance and colored-egg hatching have native adapters. Variant NBT is persistent and UUID-checked tracking snapshots synchronize appearance on the client thread, including late tracking/reconnection. Older saved coats remain temperate, and existing dyed sheep are not recolored on load.

Spring animal texture overrides are **off by default** on both targets. Opt in with the client setting `update_1_21_5.animal_appearances.enabled=true` to use UAB cow, pig, chicken and wolf textures. Leaving it off preserves vanilla and other mods' animal appearances, including custom models. This setting does not disable Spring to Life breeding, egg variants, saved animal data or other gameplay. UAB does not mutate shared vanilla model UVs.

Legacy parrot imitation retains a known audio acquisition limitation for the blaze and creeper event aliases; those two modern nested sound references are currently requested as OGG filenames. Other imitation behavior remains present, and native 1.12.2 parrots retain their own sounds. See the [modern-content matrix](MODERN_CONTENT_AUDIT.md) for the final validation checkpoint and remaining capabilities.
