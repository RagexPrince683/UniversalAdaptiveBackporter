# UAB world generation and saved structures

The content reference is released Java Edition 1.21.11. The detailed structure documentation below describes the maintained Minecraft Forge 1.7.10 target. Both native targets retain their own saves, loader and world height. These are legacy adaptations, not a claim of complete modern parity.

## Forge 1.12.2 generation

Bastion, trail-ruin and underground starts use the native saved-structure system and the released bounded template assemblies. Each population pass writes only its own chunk, with persisted completion records preventing repeated carving, loot or entity placement. Underground admission samples native density independently, protects native mineshaft/stronghold reservations, and refuses pre-existing saved or populated terrain. Surface ruins protect native village/temple reservations; bastions protect fortresses and exclude Basalt Deltas. Decoration, ores and delayed geodes respect saved structure bounds. Outpost towers retain their 1-in-600 suitable-chunk acquisition and save surrounding cages, tents, logs and targets until each piece's own chunk first populates.

Trial Spawners use finite server-owned encounters, released normal/ominous configurations, saved participants and outstanding mob UUIDs, reward queues and cooldowns. Native client spawner logic supplies only the visual preview. Vaults retain per-player reward history, normal/ominous keys and persisted item-ejection queues. The maintained adaptations keep native world height and explicit unavailable-geometry substitutions; they do not add general waterlogging or modern cave density.

The 1.12.2 target currently supplies copper and Nether ores, lower deepslate layers or clusters, tuff clusters, ten native surface biomes, bamboo/jungle decoration, cave vines and glow lichen, cherry-tree fallback placement, warm/cold ocean climate biomes with kelp/pickles/coral decoration, saved ocean structures and buried treasure, amethyst geodes, and the four Nether biomes with their vegetation and terrain decoration. It also supplies saved bastion remnants, pillager outposts, buried trail ruins, Ancient Cities and Trial Chambers. `/uab_locate` searches the retained ocean families and eligible bastion, trail-ruin, Ancient City and Trial Chamber candidates without generating their terrain. Native woodland mansions, monuments and other existing structures retain their own generators.

Ocean temperature substitution follows native river/coastline selection and uses the same layer at terrain and biome-query resolutions. Forge owns biome registration; 1.7.10 numeric biome-allocation settings do not assign 1.12.2 IDs. Existing chunk biome data and registered identities remain intact. Generation switches select only new climate candidates. Native monument admission includes the new shallow/deep ocean families, and fish spawning retains its separate population controls. Reef formations and ocean plants stay inside their population chunk and yield to saved structure bounds. Seagrass keeps its independent acquisition control and the maintained twelve attempts per new Overworld chunk at native water depths; the kelp/pickle decoration switch does not disable it.

Cherry Grove, Mangrove Swamp, Pale Garden, Meadow, Grove, Snowy Slopes, Jagged Peaks, Frozen Peaks, Stony Peaks and Bamboo Jungle use the maintained climate assignments and weight three in the native Forge biome selector. Each generation control retains its registered identity when disabled. Native legacy height profiles adapt their terrain; modern mountain density generation and cave-biome replacement are not supplied. Custom trees require a fully loaded footprint and respect saved structures. Pale trees grow hanging moss, while Pale Garden ground decoration and bone meal supply pale moss. Bamboo grows in eligible jungle terrain, and dark native caves acquire glow lichen and berry-bearing vines. Moss/azalea propagate with bone meal; moss also unlocks its existing released shipwreck-supply loot entry. The maintained biome inhabitants, Creaking Hearts and naturally populated bee nests have native implementations; oak/birch sapling growth retains flower-assisted nest acquisition.

The native Nether provider retains the maintained weighted selection of Nether Wastes, Crimson Forest, Warped Forest, Soul Sand Valley and Basalt Deltas. Forge owns biome identities. The experimental Nether-biome control and individual biome generation/weight settings govern new terrain; foreign biome providers retain ownership. Native Nether density, caves, bedrock, ores and fortress generation remain in place. The surface pass adds nylium, soul sand/soil and basalt/blackstone without querying the chunk under construction. Population adds huge fungi, roots/sprouts, vines, basalt columns/pillars, delta pools and the fourteen released Nether-fossil templates. Complete placement footprints must already be loaded, and protected inventories and saved fortress bounds prevent destructive overlap. Population stays inside its own chunk. Biome fog retains the maintained fractional blending, brightness and potion handling. Planted fungi may grow in other dimensions on matching nylium; their complete footprint is checked before mutation.

Deepslate and tuff use the existing Caves & Cliffs controls, including layer/cluster mode, height, replacement preferences and dimension lists. Vanilla ores and copper retain their deepslate ore identities, harvesting rules and experience. Writes stay inside the chunk being populated and yield to saved structure bounds. Generation changes affect new terrain only.

Geodes use the maintained multi-point density and double-Perlin shell geometry, configurable outer/middle materials, and native budding amethyst. A candidate waits in saved data until every chunk in its footprint is loaded and populated; a bounded server-tick queue completes it without requesting surrounding chunks. Invalid material IDs, disabled prerequisites, protected inventories and intersecting saved structures prevent placement. Pending candidates survive restarts; this does not regenerate previously explored terrain. Bud growth retains the legacy displacement of source water rather than installing general waterlogging.

## Generation and configuration

`config/uab.cfg` provides Ancient Cities under The Wild Update and Trial Chambers under Tricky Trials, each with an independent generation switch. Both respect Generate Structures and affect new terrain only. Cities additionally require the Sculk feature switch and deepslate content. Chambers require copper, tuff and cobbled deepslate. Registrations for the new vaults, keys and decorative dependencies remain available when generation is disabled.

The underground generators support the native `ChunkProviderGenerate` Overworld, including UAB's surface-biome selection. Superflat, replacement terrain providers, Nether and End terrain are excluded. No surface biome IDs or chunk formats change. Ancient Cities select elevated native biomes; they create their own local Deep Dark cavern rather than assigning a two-dimensional Deep Dark surface biome across the whole column.

One deterministic candidate occupies each 48-chunk placement region. Its center varies within the central eight chunks; the seed selects a city or chamber. Disabled or unsuitable candidates remain empty rather than changing into another structure. Independent regions have enough separation for the 116-block assembly radius. The operator command `/uab_locate <structure>` supports discovery as described below; cartographer exploration-map trades are not implemented.

### Locating structures

Use `/uab_locate <structure>` with operator permission (level 2); press Tab to list or complete structure names. `/efr_locate` remains an alias. Run the command in the structure's dimension.

Supported names are `ancient_city`, `trial_chambers`, `woodland_mansion`, `trail_ruins`, `ocean_monument`, `shipwreck`, `ocean_ruin`, `buried_treasure`, `pillager_outpost`, `bastion_remnant` and `end_city`.

The command first finds the nearest recorded structure of that type across the dimension's saved starts, including when its generation setting is now disabled. Saved positions describe the original site; mined structures and consumed treasure are not restored. When no saved site exists, cities, chambers, mansions, trail ruins, monuments, bastions and End Cities can search two placement regions in each direction using their generation checks. Results distinguish saved sites from new-terrain candidates. Candidate placement still depends on subsequent generation and does not promise an existing structure in already explored terrain. Shipwrecks, ocean ruins, buried treasure and outposts search saved sites only because their admission depends on populated terrain or population randomness. No search generates or populates terrain. The older `/locate_endcity` command remains available.

Sculk and Nether biomes default to enabled, so fresh configurations include the prerequisites for Ancient Cities and bastions. Existing explicit disables remain in effect. All new structure generation requires new terrain and the world's Generate Structures setting.

The canonical layout is built before placement from released jigsaw templates and weighted pools. Connectors must match names, facing and, where applicable, top alignment. Contained decorations are allowed inside their owning room; unrelated piece bounds cannot overlap. Released fallback pools and closed outer connectors terminate rejected branches. Chamber mob-pool aliases are chosen once for the structure. Layouts are bounded to 256 pieces, seven city attachment levels or twenty chamber attachment levels. The legacy height window can reject a branch or a whole candidate; templates themselves are never cropped vertically.

### Height adaptation and terrain

Complete template bounds are translated into Y=8..48. The surrounding two-block terrain boundary occupies Y=6..50 at most, above native bedrock. A candidate must have solid native terrain at least eight blocks above its highest enclosing boundary across its footprint. Terrain checks call the native terrain-noise stage on an independent provider; they neither request chunks nor run population. Native mineshaft and stronghold starts are predicted with their real seeded constructors and reserved first. A city or chamber yields to an intersecting native structure.

Cities deliberately excavate a bounded cavern, retain a deepslate floor and roof, and add surrounding sculk, sensors, catalysts and naturally summoning shriekers. Sculk patches replace natural terrain and leave tile entities, reinforced deepslate and crafted structure blocks intact. Natural creature spawning is suppressed within saved city bounds. Chambers excavate the union of their rooms and connectors and add a solid tuff boundary to seal caves and fluids. Excavation completes before templates and contained decorations are placed, preventing a later room preparation pass from erasing earlier functional content. Lakes, dungeons, geodes, buried fossils, native ore/stone veins and UAB ore/deepslate blobs respect the same saved reservations. Lake checks use the origin after the native downward ground search. Natural spring columns within eight blocks of the reserved horizontal footprint are excluded above/inside the structure so immediate vanilla flow cannot enter through an unpopulated neighboring cave. Underground flowers, glow lichen, cave vines and dripstone also yield to reserved rooms.

Native `MapGenStructure` data owns the start and component identities. Layout, rotations, processors, closed connectors and completed chunks are saved. Only the current population chunk is modified. Loot and processor randomness derives from world seed and block coordinates. Chest and torch orientation comes from the template while it is being placed, including when its neighboring support chunk has not populated yet; ordinary player placement keeps native behavior. A chunk completion ledger prevents repeating placement, refilling loot, or restoring blocks players removed. A new candidate whose footprint includes an existing saved chunk is rejected. Existing areas are not regenerated, and an already saved start retains its original layout after an update.

### Other placement safeguards

New bastions yield to predicted native fortress bounds before either structure is placed. A deterministic region priority reserves sufficient space between bastion candidates. Existing saved bastions and fortresses are retained. Prediction covers native structure algorithms, not arbitrary third-party replacements.

Nether fossils require a loaded footprint, full body clearance and substantial Soul Sand/Soul Soil support. A bounded downward search selects a valid floor; fossils do not excavate walls. Buried Overworld fossils retain their intentional embedding but avoid protected tile entities, bedrock and reserved underground structures. Huge fungi validate their footprint before changing terrain. Twisting vines search down to support correctly; both Nether vine generators stop at obstacles instead of continuing beyond them. These are feature-specific rules: underground rooms are allowed to carve, while exposed bones and vegetation are not allowed to destroy surrounding terrain.

The loot evaluator preserves released weights, counts, random-chance pools, nested tables, native enchantments, potion state, bottle amplifiers and stew effects. Missing content is deferred and logged by exact namespaced identity; it is never replaced with an unrelated ingredient or reward. Existing EFR record and glow-berry identities are mapped explicitly.

### Deliberate limitations

- There is no global three-dimensional cave-biome engine, modern negative-Y terrain, or modern jigsaw/codec runtime. The local cavern, candidate distribution, vertical limits and assembly bounds are adaptations.
- The sensor relay covers moving players, wool and sneaking. It does not implement the complete modern vibration-event/frequency system for all blocks, projectiles and mobs. Existing experimental catalyst XP spreading remains disabled; this pass does not override that gate.
- Ominous encounters use released mob/reward configurations and existing UAB omen state. Modern airborne item spawners, every equipment/drop rule, modern trial-effect UI and all modern encounter edge cases are not reproduced.
- Pots retain breakable loot inventories with native cuboid geometry. Pottery sherd designs, insertion interactions and archaeology are not implemented. City Soul Fire reuses the existing native fire support and soul-sand support, blue textures, damage and non-spreading behavior. When the existing Soul Fire feature switch is disabled, city flame markers retain clearance instead of introducing spreading ordinary fire. Candle colors/counts, powder-snow physics and lectern/campfire behavior are adapted or omitted: one candle model is used, powder snow is solid snow, lecterns are decorative bookshelves, and campfire positions retain clearance. Native 1.7.10 stair corners and redstone semantics apply.
- Armor trims/smithing templates, shields, Swift Sneak, bamboo hanging signs and any other unavailable loot are deferred explicitly. The Heavy Core is placeable, but this pass does not add a mace/combat subsystem. Echo Shards do not introduce a recovery-compass subsystem.
- Arbitrary replacement terrain/structure generators need their own reservation/terrain adapters. The new generators do not assume such providers use vanilla noise or structure algorithms.
- The released city `walls/no_corners` pool references `intact_horizontal_wall_stairs_5`, which is absent from the released client. That entry is logged and skipped; the available released wall variants remain in the pool.

## Dried Ghast and Happy Ghast

A Dried Ghast contains source water in metadata bit 2; bits 0..1 remain its hydration stage. Placing it into source water or using a water bucket fills it. An empty bucket removes its contained source. Adjacent water alone does not count as waterlogging. The client renders the contained source in the native translucent water pass. This is a local block adaptation, not generalized waterlogging or a replacement fluid engine.

A server-owned tile counts loaded game ticks. Four 6,000-tick hydration intervals produce the existing Happy Ghast entity at age -24,000: a real Ghastling. Removing water loses one visual stage per 6,000 loaded ticks. Changing wet/dry direction restarts the current interval. Stage, partial hydration/dehydration time and contained water survive saving and chunk unload; unloaded time does not advance growth. Existing stage-only blocks acquire their tile lazily through normal block lookup/random ticks. Breaking drops a fresh stage-zero item and restores contained water when removed by a player. Hydration progress is not copied to the item.

The existing ageable entity owns maturation and its saved age. A Ghastling grows for 24,000 loaded living ticks; a snowball advances approximately ten percent of the remaining time, with server-owned consumption, sound and particles. One stable adult base size now drives age scaling on both sides: 0.95-block Ghastlings and four-block adults. The entity identity, harnesses and passenger behavior remain unchanged.

Honey Blocks already exist under `enableHoney`, with their own renderer, sliding/collision behavior, piston tags, honey-bottle crafting and unpacking recipes. No duplicate Honey Block or recipe identity is introduced.

## Crossbow presentation

The crossbow adapts released item-display transforms after normalizing the legacy Forge hand transforms and correcting the legacy sprite plane by a quarter turn. Player loading uses a separate two-arm motion: the support arm pulls back over the enchantment-adjusted charge duration, then both arms adopt the charged aiming pose. Pillagers synchronize loading ticks from their server-owned attack task, retain charged state in their held stack, and distinguish walking/idle holding, loading and aiming. Their retained model uses its actual arm pivot rather than the legacy biped item transform.

## Update Aquatic generation

Kelp and sea-pickle decoration, warm-ocean coral formations, buried treasure, shipwrecks and underwater ruins have independent Update Aquatic controls. New decoration stays within its current chunk. Coral formations adapt tree, shelf and branching shapes to native ocean floors rather than replacing terrain or adding a fluid engine.

Ocean structure plans are stored in `etfuturum_ocean_sites` world data and indexed by affected chunk. Released shipwrecks and warm/cold ruins are translated through explicit native block-state mappings. Plans require an already loaded footprint whose neighboring chunks have not been populated; each chunk is clipped and placed once. This conservative admission can skip sites under unusual generation order. Placement never forces neighboring chunks or regenerates old terrain. Cold masonry overlays do not repeat the primary piece's loot or drowned.

Buried treasure stores once-only chest locations in `etfuturum_ocean_treasure`. Released loot includes a Heart of the Sea when conduits are enabled. Consumed loot and mined structure blocks survive revisiting and restart. Dolphin guidance uses nearby saved treasure and ocean sites, with the existing saved-monument fallback. Exploration-map rewards remain deferred; no map or treasure search generates terrain.

## Woodland mansions and trail ruins

The **Exploration Update / Woodland mansions** switch generates new mansions in native Roofed Forest and its mutated variant. Mansion candidates use 80-chunk regions with a 20-chunk separation margin. The released room-grid algorithm assembles all 73 available mansion templates, including mirrored rooms, connecting corridors, stairs, outer walls and roofs. Illager/allay data markers use the existing server-owned entities; spider rooms retain a native spider spawner. Furniture, chest loot and Vex templates are translated at their native boundaries.

The **Trails & Tales / Trail ruins** switch depends on Archaeology and Brushes. Candidates use 34-chunk regions with an eight-chunk separation margin, in native coniferous, birch-forest and jungle terrain. Released tower/road/house pools assemble the 84 available templates, with a seven-attachment depth, 120-piece limit and 80-block assembly radius. The root begins fifteen blocks below the native surface. Released degradation rules and per-piece capped suspicious-gravel selections are prepared once from deterministic randomness, independently of chunk visitation order. Unimplemented campfire and grindstone decorations are omitted; they are not replaced with unrelated workstations.

Both generators support the native Overworld terrain provider and Generate Structures. They sample native terrain without requesting chunks, yield to predicted village/temple bounds, reject footprints containing saved or previously populated chunks, and place only the current chunk. Mansions use the minimum sampled corner height, reject sea-level/steep sites and add native cobblestone foundations. The saved `UABSurfaceStructures` starts and `UABSurfacePiece` components retain layout, rotation, mirror, processor identity and completed chunks. Reloading does not refill loot or restore mined structure blocks. Provider replacements require a dedicated adapter. Mansion explorer-map trades are not supplied by the legacy profession system.

Archaeology extends the existing saved ocean-ruin implementation rather than introducing another ocean generator. Desert wells receive buried suspicious sand; native desert pyramids gain a small excavation pocket beside the retained treasure/TNT chamber. These are bounded native placement adaptations, not modern terrain-engine or exact natural-distribution parity.
